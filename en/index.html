Why lateral move?

  • X-frame can translate in any direction
  • Rover navigates by "turn, then drive"
  • Shows need the heading held (LED pattern, fountain, audience)
  • Open since 2018: ArduPilot issue #9352
  • Goal: Guided/Auto position target + fixed heading + lateral velocity
