Why lateral move?

  • X-frame can translate in any direction
  • Rover navigates by "turn, then drive"
  • Shows need the heading held
  • Open since 2018: ArduPilot issue #9352
  • Goal: fixed heading + lateral velocity
0:50 — Pivot-then-go looks wrong in choreography and wastes time. LED pattern, fountain and audience direction all depend on heading. Goal: Guided/Auto position target + fixed heading + lateral velocity. Issue #9352 (rmackay9, open since 2018): point the vehicle in any direction while it proceeds to the waypoint in Auto or Guided. Manual and Acro already move laterally on omni frames; the new part is Auto and Guided with the heading held.

  • 1. AR_PosControl::init reset the fixed heading → lateral output stalled
  • 2. Pivot turn fought the fixed heading
0:55 — One diagram: Guided/Auto position target → waypoint nav (fixed heading) → position controller (fwd / lat split) → lateral PID → motors. AI support helped with the Rover code and the changes; we checked them on the real boat. Usage: in Auto a DO_SET_YAW (MAV_CMD_CONDITION_YAW) command sets the fixed heading; in Guided set bit 7 of GUID_OPTIONS; omni frames only. Two pitfalls: init reset the fixed heading; the pivot turn fought it. The third (no lateral feed-forward) comes back on the Why slow? slide.

On-water test: slow, and short of the target

0:30 — Watch the boat, compared with forward moves: sideways it is about half as fast, and it tends to stop short of the target. Three boats followed the lateral program; end residuals 0.30 / 1.18 / 0.40 m.

It also stops short

  • "Reached" fires 0.3–1.8 m before the target
  • Reason: WP_RADIUS = 2 m
  • Then lateral drive is cut and the boat switches to loiter
0:30 — WpDist is the real distance to the destination while navigating; after "reached" it measures the distance to the loiter point. So the earlier 22/22 reached did not mean the boat arrived. Next: test with a small WP_RADIUS.

Why slow?

Likely cause — not verified yet

  • No lateral feed-forward (lateral_base = 0)

Logs: fixed heading is set correctly, but no PIDL log and ATT.DesYaw = 0 → tracking can't be seen

0:25 — We can't prove it, because the lateral PID is not logged. Top suspect: no cruise-throttle feed-forward on the lateral axis. Other suspects (ATC_LAT_P/I, shared ATC_ACCEL_MAX) are Q&A only. Next: add the PIDL log, retest with a small WP_RADIUS, then tune.
